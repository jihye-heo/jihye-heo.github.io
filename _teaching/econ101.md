## Interactive Learning Games
Hands-on simulations for exploring key economic concepts through decision-making.

---

### 1. 🌮 [Taco Truck: Perfect Competition Simulator](https://jihye-heo.github.io/econ-teaching-games/taco-truck-game.html)
Run your own taco truck and learn how firms behave in perfect competition.
- Price-taking behavior and the shutdown rule (P vs AVC vs ATC)
- Entry, exit, and zero economic profit in the long run


---

### 2. 💰 [Second Degree Price Discrimination Simulator](https://jihye-heo.github.io/econ-teaching-games/price-discrimination.html)
Design product tiers and prices so customers self-select — and revenue is maximized.
- Basic tier pricing extracts surplus from low-value customers
- Premium pricing prevents high-value customers from "trading down"
- Three scenarios: Cloud Storage, Airline Tickets, Coffee Shop

---

### 3. 🤝 [Prisoner's Dilemma: Game Theory Simulator](https://jihye-heo.github.io/econ-teaching-games/prisoner-dilemma-game.html)
Explore strategic decision-making through the classic Prisoner's Dilemma.
- Individual rationality vs collective irrationality
- Repeated games and strategies like Tit-for-Tat
- **AI Mode** for solo practice, **Multiplayer Mode** for real-time play with classmates

---

### 4. ⚡ [Externality Economics: AI Datacenter Game](https://jihye-heo.github.io/econ-teaching-games/externality-game.html)
A multiplayer simulation of negative externalities: datacenter production strains the grid and raises electricity bills for everyone — including local households.
- Social cost vs private cost, and why firms overproduce
- 3 rounds of production decisions; grid strain raises the electricity rate (α) for firms and households alike
- **Instructor Mode** creates the room and advances phases; **Student Mode** joins with a code

**How to use:** Instructor creates a room and shares the code; students join and make production choices each round. Results after each round show firm rankings and household bills.
